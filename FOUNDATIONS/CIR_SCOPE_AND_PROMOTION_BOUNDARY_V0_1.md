# CIR Scope and Promotion Boundary v0.1

Status: `ACTIVE_RESEARCH_BOUNDARY`

## 1. Definition

Cosmic Informational Relations (CIR) is the cosmological continuation of the TIR dependency graph. Its job is not to rename existing cosmological phenomena in TIR language. Its job is to derive, constrain, falsify, or reject cosmological relations from the admitted upstream structure.

## 2. Dependency order

The required order is

\[
\text{primitive information}
\to
\text{quantum/projective carrier}
\to
\text{spatial relational geometry}
\to
\text{temporal closure}
\to
\text{spacetime metric/coframe}
\to
\text{invariant gravity}
\to
\text{cosmological dynamics}
\to
\text{observables}
\to
\text{interpretation}.
\]

An interpretation is never allowed to become a missing upstream derivation.

## 3. Promotion gates

A CIR physical claim may advance only if all relevant gates are explicit:

- **G0 — provenance:** exact source commit and parent files are pinned;
- **G1 — type:** state-space, physical-space, gauge, coordinate and invariant quantities are separated;
- **G2 — source:** physical source variables are defined without target-fitting circularity;
- **G3 — dynamics:** an equation of motion/evolution is supplied rather than a target profile being inserted;
- **G4 — gauge/invariance:** the claimed observable survives the relevant gauge/coordinate quotient;
- **G5 — dimensional closure:** units and normalization close without an arbitrary hidden scale;
- **G6 — local controls:** known GR/local limits are recovered where claimed;
- **G7 — cosmological controls:** expansion/background consistency is checked;
- **G8 — observation:** the actual observable is compared to data with provenance;
- **G9 — prospective freeze:** predictions intended as tests are frozen before evaluation and obey a no-refit rule.

A downstream PASS cannot repair an upstream OPEN gate.

## 4. Initial CIR research tracks

These are placeholders for later derivation, not current conclusions:

1. cosmological flow and expansion;
2. information/holonomy contributions to stress-energy or effective geometric response;
3. lensing and propagation observables;
4. compact-object and diffuse-dark-sector alternatives;
5. neutrino/time-sector cosmological interfaces;
6. matter/antimatter global topology hypotheses;
7. large-scale anisotropy, chirality and polarization tests.

Each track must begin by stating which TIR parent variables it consumes.

## 5. Hard firewalls

CIR explicitly forbids the following inference shortcuts:

```text
similar picture             != same mechanism
wave structure              != fuzzy dark matter
holonomy                    != physical curvature
torsion of a chosen frame   != gravity itself
coordinate flow             != new force
successful control metric   != derived microscopic source
software validation         != empirical validation
retrospective fit           != prediction
public chronological priority != proof of copying
```
