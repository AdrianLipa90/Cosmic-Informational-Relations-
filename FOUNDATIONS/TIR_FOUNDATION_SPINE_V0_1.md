## 9. What CIR is not yet allowed to claim

At foundation v0.1 the following are **not promoted**:

- a microscopic TIR source uniquely generates the physical gravitational field;
- Bloch/CP1 geometry is literally physical cosmic geometry;
- an orbital or moire representation is itself gravity;
- wave interference constitutes dark matter;
- accelerated expansion is derived from geometry alone;
- a dark-energy mechanism has passed observational tests;
- neutrinos, Majorana structure, antimatter segregation, black-hole populations, or lensing anomalies are consequences of CIR.

Those topics belong to downstream branches with their own source, dynamics, invariance, and observation gates.
