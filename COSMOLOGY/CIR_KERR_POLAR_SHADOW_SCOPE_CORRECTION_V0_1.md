## Correction

The previously committed polar Kerr photon-region gate correctly removes the free photon-orbit radius for the special case of an observer on the Kerr spin axis.

It must NOT be read as a complete Axis-of-Evil morphology model.

Reason: an exactly axial/polar observation of an axisymmetric Kerr source has no preferred azimuth about the line of sight. The source/shadow geometry is rotationally symmetric on the observer screen. It therefore cannot by itself supply a unique sky-position angle or a unique maximum-angular-momentum-dispersion axis.

## What remains valid

The polar branch remains a valid source-reduction theorem for:

- the L_z=0 spherical photon orbit;
- x_ph(chi);
- lambda_ph(chi);
- C_ph(chi);
- a scale-only source envelope conditional on the polar-screen interpretation.

Those equations are not withdrawn.

## What is reclassified

The statement

  polar Kerr photon region -> complete AoE shadow morphology

is rejected.

The correct classification is

  polar Kerr photon region -> SCALE-ONLY SPECIAL BRANCH.

## General inclined Kerr shadow

For an asymptotic stationary observer at inclination i relative to the spin axis, the shadow boundary is parameterized by the full family of unstable spherical photon orbits:

  alpha(r) = -xi(r)/sin i,

  beta(r) = +/- sqrt[
      eta(r)
      + a^2 cos^2 i
      - xi(r)^2 cot^2 i
  ].

Thus for i != 0 the shadow is a critical curve generated by a range of photon-region radii. There is no single universal r_ph that may replace the whole contour.

A full AoE-shadow theory must therefore bind a Kerr critical curve, not one polar photon radius, to the CMB low-l field.
